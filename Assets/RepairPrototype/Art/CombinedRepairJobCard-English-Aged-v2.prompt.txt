Job Title: SYSTEM FAULT CHECK AND REPAIR

A  PIPE LEAK CHECK
1. Click the leaking pipe joint to inspect it. Check for spray and rising water.
2. Take the wrench from the shared toolbox if it is not already held.
3. Close V1. Click the inspected joint again to install the sealing clamp.
4. Reopen V1 to complete the repair. Confirm that the spray stops.
B  COOLING CHECK
5. Check the temperature trend. Click the cooling joint to inspect it.
6. Switch OFF shared power using the I/O rocker beside the fuse cabinet.
7. Hold the wrench and click the inspected cooling joint again to repair it.
8. Restore shared power to complete the repair. Check that temperature falls.
C  FUSE CIRCUIT CHECK
9. If the sonar display goes dark, check shared power and the fuse circuit.
10. Switch OFF shared power. Fit the fuses: A BLUE, B YELLOW, C RED.
11. Restore shared power, then pull the TEST lever to test the circuit.
12. If the test fails, switch OFF power before rearranging. A passed test completes repair.
NOTES
Inspections and fuse work do not require the wrench.
The wrench stays in your hand after repairs. Click the toolbox again to return it.
Closing V1 or switching OFF power only isolates the system; it does not repair it.
Mark this card manually. Crossing out a step does not repair the equipment.
The master fault lamp goes out only after all active faults are repaired.
